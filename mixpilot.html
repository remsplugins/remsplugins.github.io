How it works

Track on every track, Master on the master

TRACK

On every track. Detects the instrument, then applies low cut, EQ, compression, anti-masking and kick/bass ducking with no sidechain routing.

MASTER

On the master. Style, output, multiband glue, stereo image, 4x clipper and true-peak limiter.

  1. MixPilot Track last on every track.
  2. MixPilot Master last on the master. Pick a style and an output.
  3. Play the loudest section and press ASSISTANT.
  4. Fine-tune by ear. A/B compares before and after at matched loudness.

Features

More than a preset

Free EQ bands

Up to 12 of your own, bell, shelf, notch or cut, static or dynamic, on Stereo, Mid, Side, Left or Right, with solo.

Editable EQ

Drag the EQ points on the spectrum to fine-tune what the analysis did.

Reference track

Drop in a WAV or MP3 and MixPilot targets its balance and loudness.

Mashups

The instrumental dips its mids only while the acapella sings.

Mix review

A score out of 100, frequency clashes and live tips.

Resizable

60 to 200 %, remembered for every new window.
