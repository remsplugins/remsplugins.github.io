Normal & Advanced

Two ways to play, one click apart

Normal: pick a sound and play

The panels step aside. A visualizer moves with your track, a big preset selector sits under your hands, and RANDOM deals a new sound in one click. Perfect when you want to write, not program.

  • Browse by Type, Style or Vibe without opening a menu
  • Lock a category and the arrows and RANDOM stay in it
  • Seven visualizers, plus four scenes with The Touch skin
  • Favorites, the 4 macros and the master always within reach

Advanced: the whole synth, page by page

Click ADVANCED and the full engine slides open. Six pages sit along the top: Synth, Mod Matrix, Effects, Arp, SHATTER and Breed. The Time Machine stays in view on every one of them.

  • 3 oscillators, sub and noise, 2 filters
  • 4 envelopes and 4 LFOs, with live modulation shown on every knob
  • Drag a macro, envelope or LFO onto any knob to modulate it
  • Click NORMAL to fold it all away again

3,000 presets

Find the sound by what it is, where it goes, or how it feels

Every preset is filed three ways. Pick a tab, pick a category, then flip through with the arrows or let RANDOM choose for you.

Type

What the sound is: Bass, Lead, Pad, Pluck, Keys, Acoustic, Chord, Arp, Seq, Bell, Brass, Strings, Vox, Atmosphere, FX, Drums, Granular, Spectral…

Style

The music it was made for: House, Techno, Trance, EDM, Synthwave, Disco & Funk, Hip-Hop & Trap, Drum & Bass, Dubstep, Ambient, Cinematic, Pop, Lo-Fi, Acid, Electro.

Vibe

The mood or the record it recalls: Acid Squelch, Big Room Drop, Cold Wave, Cosmic Laser, Detroit Machine, Disco Strut, Future Noir, Night Drive and many more.

Type › Pad: every pad in the library, with its tags.

RANDOM, one click, a new sound

Stuck? Press RANDOM and Revolution deals a preset. Don't like it, press again. It's the fastest way to stumble on the sound your track was missing.

Lock it to a category

Click the padlock and RANDOM and the arrows stay inside the category you chose. Lock Pad and you only get pads, lock Synthwave and every throw of the dice stays in the 80s.

A browser built for speed

Search by name or tag, filter with tags like Mono, Unison, Slow Attack or Wide, star your favorites, and audition with the arrow keys while you scroll.

Visualizers

Seven ways to watch your sound

In Normal mode the screen is a visualizer that listens to what you play. Step through them with the arrows in the top corner. Each one takes the colours of your skin.

Spectrum: the frequency bars of your sound, with the waveform running through them.

The Touch specialSKIN + SCENES

The Touch: four stages instead of a visualizer

Choose The Touch skin and Revolution turns black and gold. In Normal mode the visualizers make way for four hand-made scenes that play along with you.

Choose a skin from the menu next to SAVE, your choice is remembered. Try them here.

New

SHATTER

Break your sound into rhythm

SHATTER slices what you play in time with the track and throws glitch effects at it, step by step. Draw on the rings, hit DICE, and a held pad turns into a beat.

StutterRepeats a slice, faster and faster
RewindPlays the sound backwards
ScrambleSwaps slices around, some reversed
BrakeSlows down to a stop, like a tape
SmearFreezes and stretches the sound
ChopRhythmic volume cuts
GritBit and sample-rate reduction
SweepResonant filter sweep
One ring per effect

Draw on a ring to light steps, right-click to clear it. Up to 16 steps on a 1/16 grid, or any other division. The centre pad switches SHATTER on.

Dice, Evolve, Wipe, Hold

DICE writes a new pattern, EVOLVE changes it a little at a time, WIPE clears it, HOLD freezes it while you play. Eight pattern slots to switch between.

Five knobs to tame it

Slice, Chaos, Backwards and Mix shape the effect, Shatter Mix blends it with the dry sound.

Breed

Two presets in, eight new sounds out

Pick two parents, set the mutation and press BREED. Eight children are born, each one test-played and level-matched, with a DNA bar that shows which genes came from A, which from B, and which mutated.

  • Choose parents from the current sound, the browser, or at random
  • Lock oscillators, envelopes, effects, filters, modulation or arp so they always come from A
  • Auto preview plays each child as you click it
  • Save a child, or send it back as a new parent for the next generation

Pump

The sidechain pump, built in

That breathing, ducking movement of dance music, without routing a kick to a compressor. Pump sits with the effects and moves your sound in time with the host.

  • Five shapes: Pump, Soft, Tri, Saw, Gate, plus Flip to turn them over
  • Synced to the host tempo, from long swells to fast 1/16 chops
  • Depth, Curve, Smooth, Sens, Mix and Out to dial it in

Time Machine

One slider, fifty years of sound

Slide it back and the whole synth ages with it: tape, 12-bit converters, rave samplers, the colour of the gear of each era. Press NOW to come straight back to today. It sits at the top of every page, so you can try it on any sound.

Custom LFO

Draw the movement you hear in your head

Each of the four LFOs can play a shape you draw yourself. Open the editor, sketch it, and the sound follows the line. Then drag the cross onto any knob to modulate it.

Curve

Shape it point by point

Click to add a point, drag to move it, double-click to remove it. Bend any segment into a curve, or hold Shift and draw freehand. The grid snaps to the beat for rhythmic shapes.

SineTriangleSaw UpSaw DownSquareStepsPumpBounceRandom

Nine starting shapes, plus clear, undo and redo.

Orbit

One loop, two movements

Draw a closed shape and a dot runs round it. Its height drives the LFO, its left-right position drives a second source, LFO X. Send them to cutoff and pan, and a single orbit moves both in a way no classic LFO can.

CircleFigure 8TriangleSquareStar

Five starting orbits to bend into your own.

Filters

Two filters with an analog soul

Two filters, each with 12 types modelled on the circuits behind the classic sounds. Run them in series to sculpt, or in parallel to blend two characters. Push the drive and they growl, open the resonance and the acid diode screams.

Ladder

LP24, LP12, BP, HP. Warm 70s mono bass and leads.

Diode LP

The squelchy acid line sound.

SVF

LP, BP, HP, Notch. Clean and precise for pads and keys.

Comb + / −

Metallic, flanged and plucked tones.

Formant

Vowel sounds that make a synth talk.

Per filter

Cutoff, resonance, drive, envelope, keytrack, mix.

And more

Everything else inside

Mod Matrix

16 slots, each with source, amount and destination, next to the four macros.

Arpeggiator

A 16-step pattern with gate and accent per step, swing, octaves and latch.

Effects

Distortion, compressor, Juno-style chorus, phaser, delay, reverb, tape, EQ and Pump, all on one page.

Vital & Serum 2 import

Drop a .vital or .SerumPreset file and play it, wavetables and macros included.

4 engines

Wavetable, granular with your own samples, spectral with up to 96 partials, sampler.

16 unison voices

Per oscillator, with detune and spread for huge supersaws.

MPE

Per-note bend, pressure and slide from an MPE controller.

Everywhere

VST3, AU, LV2 and Standalone on Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux.
