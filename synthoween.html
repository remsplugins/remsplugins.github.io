What lurks insideEvery panel of the plug-in

Every sound is synthesized by the plug-in itself: formant voices for the screams, string clusters, additive bells, friction for the creaking door. No sample library to install, nothing to license.

Sample

25 spooky sounds. START, END, CROSSFADE and TUNE carve the one you pick. CROSSFADE at 0 plays it once, turn it up and it loops forever.

Filter

Low pass 12 and 24 dB, high pass, band pass, notch, plus two cursed ones: Demon Vowel makes the sound speak, Haunted Comb turns it into a rusty metal pipe.

Amp & LFO

An ADSR envelope, and an LFO with 7 shapes, including Heartbeat (lub-dub) and Chaos.

Cursed FX

Reverb, Haunted Hall, Possession (demon voice), Bone Crusher, Grave Tape. Then Delay, Banshee Echo, Reverse Spectre, Heartbeat Gate and Ring of Hell, synced to your tempo.

Modulation

2 slots on the main page and 4 more in MATRIX, with a second envelope, glide and pitch bend range. Route the LFO, envelopes, velocity, mod wheel or pure randomness.

Trick or Synth

Click the carving at the bottom right of the plug-in and a random sound crawls out of the grave. Keep it, save it, or summon another.

Summoning ritualHow to install

macOS

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy SynthOWeen.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and SynthOWeen.component (AU) to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
  3. Or double-click fix_mac_plugins.command: it installs, unblocks and rescans the plug-in for you.
  4. Restart your DAW and rescan. In Ableton Live: Settings → Plug-Ins → Rescan.

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy the SynthOWeen.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan. In Ableton Live: Options → Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan.

If macOS blocks it

The plug-in is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may quarantine it. Paste this into Terminal, then restart your DAW:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SynthOWeen.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SynthOWeen.component
