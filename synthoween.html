What lurks inside

Every sound in Synth'O'Ween is conjured by the plug-in itself: formant voices for the screams, string clusters, additive bells, friction for the creaking door. No sample library to install, nothing to license, nothing to clear for your videos.

  • Sample: 25 spooky sounds. START, END, CROSSFADE and TUNE shape the one you pick. CROSSFADE at 0 plays it once, turn it up and it loops forever.
  • Filter: low pass 12 and 24 dB, high pass, band pass, notch, plus two weird ones. Demon Vowel makes the sound speak, Haunted Comb turns it into a metal pipe.
  • LFO: 7 shapes, including Heartbeat and Chaos.
  • Modulation: 2 slots on the main page and 4 more in MATRIX, with a second envelope, glide and pitch bend range.
  • Trick or Synth: click the ghoul at the bottom right and a random nightmare is born.

The summoning ritual

macOS

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy SynthOWeen.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and SynthOWeen.component (AU) to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
  3. Or double-click fix_mac_plugins.command: it installs, unblocks and rescans the plug-in for you.
  4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Settings → Plug-Ins → Rescan.

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy the SynthOWeen.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Options → Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan.

If macOS blocks it

The plug-in is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may lock it away. Paste this into Terminal, then restart your DAW:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SynthOWeen.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SynthOWeen.component
