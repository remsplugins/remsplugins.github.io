SampleTuned

Your sample library, sorted by key and by type on one colour map. Hover a sound to hear it, click to copy it, drag it straight into Ableton.

VST3StandalonemacOSWindows 10/11LinuxFree
Download See what it does 0 downloads

Pick a key on the wheel or the grid. Compatible keys (relative and fifth) light up too.

Key on the wheel

Keys sit on the circle of fifths, Camelot style. Each key gets its own colour, with a smooth gradient between neighbours.

Type in rings

Drums sit in the centre, then bass and 808, then synths, pads, keys, vocals and FX on the outside.

One-shot or loop

A new filter tells hits from loops, from the file name or from the sound itself.

Keyless sounds too

Hi-hats, claps and textures have no key. They stay in the centre, and you can show or hide them.
